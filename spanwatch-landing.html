Where The Signal Comes From

No single source tells
the whole story. We watch all of them.

Heavy movable projects intelligence is scattered across symposia, federal registers, state lettings, and maritime permitting. HMS Project Monitor consolidates over 35 different sources into one feed.

Heavy Movable Structures, Inc.

The biennial Movable Bridge Symposium and its technical paper archive name owners, designers, and project specifics years ahead of construction.

heavymovablestructures.org/symposium

U.S. Coast Guard Bridge Program

Any new or altered bridge over a navigable waterway requires a USCG permit — one of the earliest public signals a project exists.

dco.uscg.mil/Office-of-Bridge-Programs

State DOT Letting & Bid Systems

NYSDOT, TxDOT, FDOT, LADOTD, IDOT and other state letting calendars publish upcoming construction contracts, including movable spans.

NYSDOTTxDOTFDOTLADOTDIDOT

FHWA Bridge Programs & NBI

The Bridge Investment Program and National Bridge Inventory flag funded replacements and condition-driven projects nationwide.

fhwa.dot.gov/bridge/nbi

County & Municipal Capital Plans

Multi-year capital improvement plans (like county bridge replacement programs) disclose movable structure projects well before bidding.

Cape May County, NJ

ENR & Dodge Construction Network

Engineering News-Record's Top Design Firms rankings and Dodge's pre-planning project data track who's designing what, and where.

enr.comconstruction.com

Trade & Engineering Publications

Roads & Bridges, Bridge Design & Engineering, and regional construction trades cover awards and design milestones as they happen.

roadsbridges.combridgeweb.com

Engineering Firm Newsrooms

Direct monitoring of project-win announcements and staffing moves from the specialist firms themselves.

Hardesty & HanoverModjeski and Masters

SAM.gov Federal Contract Opportunities

Catches Army Corps of Engineers, federal lands, and military movable structure work that never appears in state DOT letting systems.

sam.gov/opportunitiesUSACE opportunities

USACE Civil Works (Locks & Dam Gates)

District-level navigation lock and dam gate rehabilitation programs are heavy movable structures categorized as civil works, not bridges — and tracked separately from highway sources.

usace.army.mil/Navigation-Locks

Class I Railroad Bridge Programs

CSX, Norfolk Southern, Union Pacific, and BNSF own a large share of US swing and lift bridges, disclosed through STB filings and railroad capital programs rather than ENR or DOT lettings.

stb.govCSX bridgesProgressive Railroading

Port Authority Capital Plans

Port authorities often own or co-fund movable bridges and flood gates near their channels, disclosed in multi-year capital plans distinct from county and municipal CIPs.

Port Authority of NY/NJPort of Houston

USACE Section 408 & NEPA Filings

Permission requests and environmental review filings often name project scope and design firm before a USCG bridge permit is filed.

usace.army.mil/Section408

State QBS Award Announcements

Qualifications-Based Selection shortlists and awards for bridge design are posted on state procurement portals separately from construction lettings, catching the design firm assignment earlier.

FDOTNJTPAMassDOT

Flood Control & Dam Gate Districts

Water management districts and regional flood control agencies fund movable gate replacements on their own capital schedules, entirely outside any bridge-monitoring channel.

South Florida WMD

USDOT Discretionary Grant Awards

Bridge Investment Program, INFRA, Mega, and RAISE award announcements name specific movable bridge projects and dollar amounts the moment they're funded — often a year or more ahead of any letting.

USDOT briefing roomBridge Investment Program

Bridge & Toll Commissions

Agencies like the Burlington County Bridge Commission own and operate movable spans directly, with their own capital plans separate from state DOT systems.

Burlington County Bridge Commission

Bureau of Reclamation

The USACE counterpart for irrigation and hydroelectric dams in the western US — covers dam gate rehabilitation work outside USACE's own civil works program.

usbr.gov/assetmanagement/dams

FRA CRISI Grant Awards

Federal Railroad Administration discretionary grants increasingly fund rail bridge rehabilitation, a funding channel distinct from the Class I railroads' own capital disclosures.

railroads.dot.gov/grants/crisi

NSBA/AISC Prize Bridge Awards

Biennial steel bridge awards name the owner, design firm, and fabricator for movable bridges — useful for backfilling projects that slipped past every other channel.

aisc.org/nsba/prize-bridge-awards

Marine Log

Maritime industry trade publication covering port infrastructure, USACE navigation projects, and waterway construction — a parallel signal stream to Waterways Journal for lock and dam gate activity.

marinelog.com

Waterways Council

The leading inland waterways industry advocacy organization tracks USACE civil works funding requests, Water Resources Development Act provisions, and navigation lock modernization priorities.

waterwayscouncil.org

HigherGov Federal Intelligence

Aggregates federal contract awards and grant data from SAM.gov, FPDS, and USASpending with advanced filtering — useful for catching movable bridge and gate fabrication awards the moment they're posted.

highergov.com

USASpending.gov / FPDS

The official federal spending transparency database records every federal contract award by NAICS code and agency — the authoritative source for finding USACE and DOT bridge and gate fabrication contracts by dollar amount and recipient.

usaspending.govfpds.gov

Federal Permitting Dashboard

The FAST-41 permitting transparency portal lists major infrastructure projects undergoing federal environmental review — an early signal for large movable bridge and waterway gate projects before procurement begins.

permits.performance.gov

Amtrak Capital Program

Amtrak's NEC capital investment program funds rehabilitation and replacement of dozens of movable rail bridges along the Northeast Corridor — disclosed in annual capital plans and NEPA documents well before construction contracts are let.

amtrak.com/northeast-corridornec-commission.com
